## 2026.11.16 - 2026-11-16
### Added
- Steam's game hover cards show the game's tags, under the user tags on search, bundle, curator and home pages, and under the reviews and release date on tag hubs and sale pages.
- The settings page and the userscript dialog gain a "Common questions" link to the FAQ, ahead of the feedback links.
### Fixed
- Requests to Steam no longer send the address of the store page you are on. A wishlist or search address could name your account or your search.
### Removed
- The settings and the README no longer link to an anonymous Google Forms feedback form. Report bugs and leave feedback through the GitHub issue forms linked in both.
